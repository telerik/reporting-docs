## Methodology and Validation

Markdown front matter and fenced code are excluded from prose checks. The scan recognizes Markdown headings, inline/reference-style and HTML images, inline/reference-style links, HTML and Markdown tables, typed callouts, fenced/HTML code blocks, and snippet/include directives. Absent content types score 5. The 1–5 scores use these consistent source-check bands: headings score 2 when no heading hierarchy exists or multiple levels are skipped, 3 for a single skipped level or missing H1, and are reduced for generic or empty headings; alt text scores 2/3/4 when missing alt affects at least 50%/20%/less than 20% of images, with generic alt text scored down similarly; generic/bare-URL link ratios of at least 70%/25%/less than 25% score 2/3/4; tables score 2 for missing detectable headers and 3 for empty cells; prose scores 2/3/4 when the average sentence exceeds 32/25/20 words or the share of sentences longer than 25 words exceeds 40%/25%/10%; detected color-only or bold-only warnings score 3; inconsistent or incomplete callouts score 3, multiple typed callout styles score 4; unlabeled code ratios of at least 50%/20%/less than 20% score 2/3/4, while unclear introductions reduce the score to 4. A dimension scores 5 when no issue is detected (and when its content type is absent).
Severity uses the issue examples: Critical for a missing heading hierarchy, an informative-context image without alt text, or an article whose links are all non-descriptive; High for multiple H1 headings, repeated heading skips, tables without detectable headers, or at least three unlabeled code blocks. Other detected patterns are Medium or Low as identified in the JSONL. These are conservative pattern detectors: the scan does not infer actual image content, table purpose, callout severity, or semantic link quality beyond obvious generic labels. Sentence length is not a Flesch-Kincaid grade calculation and active voice, jargon definitions, captions, and visual presentation are not judged.
Findings are source-pattern detections, not claims about rendered behavior. The weighted score for each article is the sum of its eight dimension scores multiplied by the specified weights; repository score is the mean of unrounded per-article weighted scores, displayed to one decimal place.

Validation passed: 1446 scored records = 1446 eligible articles; every record has all eight dimensions with scores in [1, 5]; each weighted score was recalculated and matched its recorded value. 1446 articles were scored; none failed processing.
